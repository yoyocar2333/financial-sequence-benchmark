# Revised conference abstract

## Title

Diagnosing Apparent Predictive Gains in Financial Machine Learning

## Abstract

Financial machine-learning gains may reflect new information, alternative price representations, or probability calibration. We diagnose these channels in five-day stock-reversal forecasts using matched price, accounting, and news controls. Across AAPL and TSLA, technical-feature additions show no corrected advantage, and all ten raw accounting-fusion settings trail constant shrinkage. Calibrated gains remain uncertain; an allocation exception arises during initialization. In an Apple news cohort, density contributes most of the observed score change, while a secondary sentiment result is delay-sensitive. The integrated evaluation distinguishes measured loss improvements from stronger claims about new information, discrimination, and economic value, while preserving explicit cohort-specific statistical uncertainty.

## Keywords

financial machine learning; incremental information; probability calibration; forecast combination; news density; reversal prediction
