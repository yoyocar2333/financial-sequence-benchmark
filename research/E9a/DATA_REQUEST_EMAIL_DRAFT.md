## Draft A — Stocktwits Enterprise pilot inquiry

Subject: Academic inquiry: 30–50 historical AAPL/TSLA records with version provenance

Dear Stocktwits Enterprise Team,

We are preparing a non-commercial academic study of whether financial text adds predictive information beyond prices and probability shrinkage. Before considering a larger dataset, we would like to understand the availability, research rights and cost of a small historical provenance pilot.

Could you provide, or describe an authorized way to review, 30–50 AAPL/TSLA records spanning several historical years? Where lawfully available, we would value examples covering edits and deletion handling, as well as ordinary records. We do not request restoration or disclosure of content that your policies prohibit retaining.

For each record, we would need its message/version identifiers, symbol association, exact content representation or permitted review mechanism, publication/edit/deletion times, timestamp precision, and evidence tying the supplied version to a historical capture. Please distinguish source-event time, historical vendor receipt, and current export time. If historical arrival or independent version evidence is unavailable, an explicit statement would be useful.

For a possible later study, can you document at least four consecutive years for both symbols, fixed query scope, missing periods, monitoring/pagination completeness, and correction/deletion procedures? We would also appreciate the permitted retention, derived-feature, academic publication and confidential reviewer-access terms, and separate pilot and multi-year academic quotations.

We will not redistribute raw messages or user-level data. This inquiry does not authorize a paid service or data delivery; any agreement would require institutional review. Please let us know what proposal or faculty sponsorship information you require.
